- The home hero now follows the theme: cobalt on cream in Paper, cream on cobalt in Night. Scrolling away, the name sinks behind the Bromo plate.
- The dot screen is gone. The hero and the sign-in panel use the same paper grain as every other page.
- Page titles, home section headings, the hero name and the footer name are printed with the ink speckle from the stats numerals.
- Photos show their own colours on hover. Images can no longer be dragged off the page.
- The footer lines up with the header and the page; only the Hollar plate runs edge to edge.
- The header shows only the RH mark.
- The tracker board is removed; old links go to the home page.
- The résumé link is now called CV, and the old portfolio PDF link is gone.
