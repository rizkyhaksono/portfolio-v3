I rebuilt the visual system around reading instead of decoration.

- Replaced glass and operating-system-style cards with solid editorial surfaces.
- Self-hosted the display, serif, body, and mono fonts.
- Standardized spacing, borders, headings, chips, and page widths.
- Updated the public role to Senior AI Engineer across content and metadata.
- Removed animated backgrounds, visitor telemetry, and the public Twitter link.
- Added quota protection and safer failure handling to the assistant.

The goal was simple: fewer effects, less filler, and a clearer account of the work.

Version 1.5.0 replaced this monochrome system with one-ink print, Literata through next/font, and Umami analytics. The fonts and telemetry notes above describe 1.4.0 only.
