This release returns the site to the one-ink print of 1.5.0 and keeps a few things from 1.6.0.

- Brought back the print: one cobalt ink on cream paper, Literata type, and a night print for dark mode.
- Drew an RH mark in the same ink. It sits in the header and the colophon, and it is the favicon and the share image mark.
- Put my portrait at the top of the home page, large beside my name on a wide screen and under the job line on a phone.
- Made the text a little smaller, so more of each page fits on screen.
- Set the site to open in Paper, the light print. Night and System are in the menu.
- Kept the anime generator in Tools, with images by mood and random quotes.

The aim stays the same: less decoration, more reading.
