This release changes how the site looks, not how it is organised. Every page keeps its structure and its content.

- Drew a new RH monogram and used it in the header, the colophon, the favicon and the share images.
- Set the whole site in IBM Plex Sans, with IBM Plex Mono for code and the title block.
- Moved to black and white with a single orange accent, used only for focus, the current page and the reading line.
- Put my portrait in the home hero: large beside the headline on a wide screen, and in the opening section on a phone.
- Gave photos a plain frame with crop marks, and turned the colophon into a title block that shows the current revision.
- Switched to plain light and dark themes. The site now opens in light mode. On a wide screen dark is one tap away in the header; on a smaller screen the menu offers light, dark and system.
- Brought the anime generator back in Tools, with images by category and random quotes.

The aim stays the same: less decoration, more reading.
