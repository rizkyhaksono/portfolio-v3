This release replaces the monochrome system of 1.4.0 with a printed one, called Lautan Pasir after the sand sea below the Tengger caldera.

- Printed every page in one cobalt ink on cream paper. Night print flips the same tokens to cream ink on night-blue paper.
- Set the whole site in Literata, roman and italic, served through next/font.
- Gave every route one page pattern: ledgers for lists, articles with a contents rail, plates for photos, figures for numbers.
- Printed photos as one-ink duotones and added a colophon with a seal mark and an engraving after Wenceslaus Hollar.
- Added Umami analytics: reader counts on articles and projects, and a Readers section on the stats page.
- Moved feedback into the colophon and the Contents sheet, so no button floats over the text.
- Fixed site search, the RSS feed, the generated share images and certificates, and focus after closing dialogs.

The aim stays the same as 1.4.0: less decoration, more reading.
