- Set the whole site in Plus Jakarta Sans, by the Jakarta type foundry Tokotype. Headings now step down by weight and size instead of italic.
- The home page opens on a printed plate: my name in cream on a dotted cobalt sky, with Bromo and the Tengger sand sea below, after an 1869 lithograph by Johan Conrad Greive and Abraham Salm. The mountains are re-cut as line engraving, and their peaks rise in front of the letters. The plate stays the same in Paper and Night.
- The opening sentence and the links to projects, writing and the résumé now sit just under the plate, and the sentence still inks itself as you scroll.
- The sign-in panel gets the same dotted ink, and share images use the new type.
- The Hollar landscape stays at the foot of every page.
- Fixed the article styles (headings, lists, tables, code and quotes) that 1.7.1 dropped by mistake.
